About the project

Built so no one has to search for help while help is needed.

HyperAid began as a simple observation: in an emergency, people lose minutes not to distance, but to indecision - which number to call, which hospital is closest, what to do until help arrives. We built one place that answers all three.

Our Mission

Cut the time between "something is wrong" and "the right help is on its way" to as close to zero as possible, using AI as a calm, always-available guide rather than a replacement for real responders.

Our Vision

A world where every phone doubles as an emergency dispatcher: locating services, coordinating contacts, and giving trustworthy first-response guidance in the local language.
